03 — About Us

We stock what the terraces remember

Retro Jersey started as one wardrobe of match-worn and reissued shirts, collected shirt by shirt from flea markets, old kit rooms and collectors who trusted us with a piece of their season. Today it's a small archive, still handled the same way: checked for weight, print and cut before it goes on the rail.

No dropshipping, no stock photos of shirts we don't have. If it's listed, it's folded on our shelf in Marrakesh, ready to ship or hand over in person.
